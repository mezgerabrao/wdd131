Site Name

Identity & Branding

AIGrowthHub

AIGrowthHub is a focused, professional name that immediately communicates the site's two core themes: Artificial Intelligence and Business Growth. The word "Hub" signals a curated resource center rather than a personal blog, lending it authority and scalability.

The name is short, memorable, easy to spell, and works well across all branding contexts — from browser tab titles to social sharing. It appeals directly to Product Managers, Growth Marketers, and digital strategists looking for practical AI resources.

Optional domain: aigrowthhub.io

Site Purpose

Scope & Services

AIGrowthHub is a resource website that explores how Artificial Intelligence is transforming Product Marketing and Growth strategies in modern digital businesses. The site provides:

  • An overview of key AI concepts applied to marketing and product growth
  • A curated, filterable catalog of AI tools used by marketing and growth teams
  • Practical examples of AI-powered strategies such as personalization, predictive analytics, automation, and experimentation
  • A newsletter sign-up and contact form for visitors interested in staying updated on the topic

The site targets Product Managers, Growth Marketers, and digital business leaders who want to understand and apply AI without needing a technical background.

Scenarios

Target Audience Questions

The following questions represent real needs of the site's target visitors and guide the content decisions across all pages:

"What AI tools can I use today to improve my marketing campaigns without a technical team?"

"How are other companies using AI for growth experimentation and personalization? Where can I see real examples?"

"I'm a Product Manager — how does AI fit into my roadmap and customer engagement strategy?"

"Where can I sign up to receive updates and resources about AI in digital marketing?"

Typography

Fonts & Usage

Two Google Fonts are used to create a clear hierarchy: a strong heading font for impact and a highly readable body font for content comfort.

Font 1 – Lato (Headings & UI)
AI-Powered Growth Starts Here
Weight: 900 (headings) / 700 (subheadings) / 400 (labels)
Usage: H1–H3 headings, navigation links, button text, section labels, logo
Font 2 – Open Sans (Body & Content)
Artificial Intelligence is reshaping how product teams acquire, engage, and retain customers. This site explores practical strategies, tools, and examples for modern digital growth.
Weight: 400 (body) / 600 (emphasis)
Usage: Paragraphs, card descriptions, form labels, footer text, list items
